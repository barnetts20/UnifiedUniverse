## 2. Verified
All offline, in `Verification/REPORT.md` (generated by `Tools/UniVerify/verify_all.py`). Images are in `Verification/images/`, and what I saw in them is in `Verification/OBSERVATIONS.md`.

- **Coordinates (Tier 1).** 10^6 fuzz cases, zero mismatches across `UniCoord.h`, `UniCoord.ush` (run on the CPU through the shim), `__int128` and a Python big-int oracle. The oracle was mutation-tested: 9 of 9 planted corruptions caught. ToHiLo worst error is 2^-49 (limit 2^-46). Borrow chains, root wrap, antipode, extreme `AddDisplacement` and hex round trip all pass. g++, clang, and a strict C++20 `-Wshadow` build are all clean.
- **Seeds.** Chi-squared inside the 99% band for sequential inputs on every seed path. ProcSeed's recorded lesson is reproduced: without Avalanche, chi-squared is 1.26e7. Object seeds have zero collisions over 300 cells × 2^15 (D-109). A Python oracle agrees on 2,007 cases.
- **Windows and levels.** Zero slot-bijection, stability or diff violations over 5,184 slides at N = 1..6, including the root seam (D-100). Level coverage: zero violations in 240k trials. f(r) and photometry are within their float bounds (D-110).
- **Generation (Tier 2), on two scenes (cosmic web → galaxies; galaxy → stars).** Determinism: 54,864 cells, 0 differ across histories and evict-and-revisit. Counts: z = −0.88 over 12,735 tasks. Slicing conserves N_total to 0.2%. Fidelity: r = 0.92 (galaxy disc) and 0.98 (web). Coverage: 416k children, 0 outside their contributor. Slice draws: 24 slices, 0 outside. The budget is under the D-005 caps. The baked UniVol path (format, mips as exact means, mip-descent placement r = 0.999, 48 tile symmetries) also passes.
- **Runtime streamer.** The asynchronous probe → allocate → place protocol, with a CPU backend, reproduces the reference pipeline cell for cell: 5,375 cells and 719,687 objects identical (T2.stream.parity).
- **Rendering (Tier 3).** Jitter: worst 2.6e-3 px with the camera parked at 1e30, 1e35 and 2^127 cm and stepped 0.001 cm per frame (limit 0.01). The design's hi/lo form is 1.6e4 px off for near objects in coarse cells, which is why D-104 changed it. Ordering: 0 reversals in 100k pairs (per-depth compression gets 2,284 wrong). Occlusion: 0 wrong in 20k placements. Flythrough from 3 Gpc to 1 m above a star: **0 pops** among 545,902 entering objects, after fixing three real pop sources it found (D-116, D-117, D-118).
- **Static UE checks.** Shader cores stay in the shared subset. Every GPU struct is asserted to a multiple of 16. Render hooks are in one file. No UE includes in UniCore.

## 4. Needs your decision
- **Decided 2026-11-01 (owner):** D-133 option B (N_total 2.69e10, star counts × 0.134), D-132 keep the slices, D-131 r_H = c/H₀, D-118 accept N_eff = 1.75. The items below are kept for the record.
- **D-118 (HIGH reroll): level assignment uses N_eff = N − 1/K = 1.75, not N = 2.** The design's claim that objects stream in where they are already invisible does not hold with N = 2: the window lags the camera by one frame, and the flythrough measured pops. With 1.75 there are 0 pops. Cost: about 1.5× residency. The alternative is to keep N = 2 and accept fade-band pops. Decide before any content is authored.
- **D-107 / D-109 (HIGH): seeds.** ProcSeed's recorded statistics match the *fast* `HashCombine` form, not the Jenkins mix, so the port defaults to that. UE-2 decides. Separately, the design's object seed `MixSeed(CellSeed, index)` collided 5–260× above the birthday rate, so cell and object seeds use an injective mixer instead (D-109). That defines all generated content.
- **D-104: exact 128-bit per-cell deltas** replace the design's hi/lo floats for project-and-cull (measured: hi/lo is 1.6e4 px off near bright stars). This is render-only, but it is a design change.

## 8. Afternoon addendum: volumetric raymarching (branch `uu/volumetrics`)
*Added at your request ("devise our density raymarching scheme"). It is on its own branch on top of day 1, so tonight's §3 test is unaffected: install day 1 first, test it, then apply the volumetrics patch if you want to look at this.*

- **Design:** `Docs/VOLUMETRICS.md`, also in the claude.ai project. **The volume draws exactly the light the sprites do not.** That is per emitter, from the same field, slices, magnitude draws and fades that placement uses, so sprites plus volume always add up to the total (D-128). It makes every handoff seamless by construction: galaxy sprite → glow → resolved stars, level fades, velocity-LOD gaps, and sensitivity.
- **Built offline:**
  - the shared core `Shaders/Private/Core/UniVolume.ush` (carriers, log-uniform march with per-field step hints, interval union, prefiltering, dust, transmittance slices);
  - the CPU builder `Source/UniCore/UniVolume.h` (unresolved-light tables, per-frame LUTs, carriers from resident contributors with exact 128-bit deltas, luminosity budget);
  - the reference renderer, and 10 new checks.
- **Verified (Tier 3, all green):**
  - The march agrees with a brute-force march within 0.8% (emission) and 0.0013 (optical depth), at 85 steps per ray on average.
  - Conservation over the whole sphere: −0.16%, −0.14% and −0.10% at Δm = −1, 0 and +2 (z < 1).
  - Galaxy sprite → carrier handoff: continuous within 0.84%.
  - Nested galaxy-in-web: exact.
  - Translation invariance at 2^126 cm: bit-identical.
  - The extragalactic background is finite at 27.43 mag/arcsec² and converged within 0.01%.
  - Flythrough: no truncated rays.
  - Images: `still_vol_*` and `flythrough_volume_contact_sheet.png`.
- **What the checks caught and fixed:** sub-pixel carriers were mis-integrated by 12–17% with one ray per pixel; they are now supersampled where needed, at bounded cost. The step hints collapsed on the galactic axis. A background prefilter never engaged, which cost 500 steps per ray.
- **Needs your decision:**
  - **D-133, the luminosity budget.** The demo galaxy's 2e11 stars add up to M = −23.18, but the galaxy is drawn at −21.00 (7.4×). With the volume this shows as the galaxy brightening 7.4× as you fly in. The fix rerolls star content: N_total = 2.69e10, a fainter stellar function, or a different `NTotalRefMag`. The checks use a corrected copy; the demo itself is unchanged.
  - **D-131:** the horizon dimming value (r_H = c/H₀).
  - **D-132:** transmittance slices in v1.
- **Changes that reach the day-1 renderer** (render-only, no content change, all three in `UniProject.ush`):
  - Extended objects with children hand their sprite light to the volume over 4 → 8 px (D-129).
  - All sprites get horizon dimming, which is under 1% inside 50 Mpc (D-131).
  - Sprite colour is normalised by luminance instead of by its brightest channel, so light sums conserve (D-135).
- **Background marching (evening follow-up, D-136).** The background (the web's unresolved galaxies) now starts at 0.1 Mpc. It is marched into a 6-layer direction map, re-marched a few faces per frame only when the camera has moved enough or its LUT changes, and composited per pixel as `B_near + T_near × B_map`. T3.vol.cube: total 0.000%, 99th-percentile pixel ≤ 0.02% against marching everything per pixel.
- **Streamer fade rule (D-128 amendment).** A level's fade now starts at 0 and cannot rise while any cell in its window is still generating. A teleport fill therefore shows as glow first, then the stars fade in together, instead of patchy pop-in. T2.stream.volume_parity: the streamer's volume scene is byte-identical to the reference, with 0 violations.
